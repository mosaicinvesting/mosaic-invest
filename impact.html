How we measure this

These numbers come directly from the curriculum pages at mosaicinvesting.org. There's no login and no way to trace a response back to a person: every check-in is anonymous and always skippable. A "start" is logged the first time someone opens a course, and a "complete" is logged once every checkpoint question on that course has been answered. Confidence is a single self-rated number, 1 to 10, asked once at each point. It's a light-touch signal, not a scientific study, and we'd rather show honest, small numbers than none at all.
